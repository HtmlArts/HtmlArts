02 / The concept Plain language

What is the attention economy?

It is an environment where human attention is scarce, valuable, and fiercely competed for.

What is scarce?

Your time, focus, curiosity, and mental energy. A single day has only so many waking hours.

What is valuable?

The opportunity to show you a message, an ad, or a recommendation that alters what you do next.

Core principle: When attention creates commercial value, interfaces are engineered to capture it—and often to hold it.

06 / Two sides Trade-offs matter

Attention is not inherently the enemy.

When it helps

Learning platforms, critical alerts, intellectual communities, creative discovery, and thoughtful craft earn attention for good reason.

When it harms

Sensational alerts, infinite auto-playing feeds, manufactured outrage, and opaque hooks siphon attention from life priorities.

The question is never “How much attention?”
It is “Attention in service of what?”

Thoughtful design respects human agency, makes stopping frictionless, and creates value beyond time on screen.

Evaluate outcomes, not just metrics Folio 06
07 / System behavior Measure the right thing

What gets measured shapes what gets built.

If an engineering team measures aggregate session duration as its north star, it inevitably optimizes for prolonged sessions—even when a 30-second visit best serves the reader.

A proxy metric is not the objective. Pair engagement telemetry with reader agency, satisfaction, and the grace to disengage.
